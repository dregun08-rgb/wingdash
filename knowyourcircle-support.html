How to Play

1. The Setter picks a question pack and confirms their real answers on their phone

2. They share a 6-character room code with the Guesser

3. The Guesser enters the code on their phone and has 30 seconds per question to guess the truth

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. The app uses anonymous authentication.

Is it free? Yes, completely free.

How do I delete my data? Uninstall the app to remove all local data.
