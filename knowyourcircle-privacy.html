Know Your Circle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Developer: WOW Studios (Everette Egun)

Information We Collect

Know Your Circle collects minimal data to provide the game experience:

  • Display name you enter (stored locally on your device)
  • Game room data (questions, answers, scores) stored temporarily on Firebase for multiplayer functionality
  • Anonymous authentication ID (no email or personal info required)

How We Use Your Data

Data is used solely to enable multiplayer gameplay between devices. Room data is temporary and not used for advertising or analytics.

Data Storage

Game data is stored on Google Firebase servers. Local profile data (name, XP, streak) is stored on your device only.

Third Party Services

We use Google Firebase for real-time multiplayer functionality and anonymous authentication.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13.

Data Deletion

You can delete your local data by uninstalling the app. Game room data is automatically deleted from our servers periodically.
